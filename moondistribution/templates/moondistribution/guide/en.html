1. What is it?

Every few months the alliance re-distributes its moons. Corps apply for the moons they want, the Auth rates every corp by its activity and assigns the moons automatically and transparently. {% if s.exclude_r64 %}R64 moons stay with the alliance and are not distributed.{% endif %}

2. Timeline
  1. The Mining Officers prepare a round and open it.
  2. From then on applications are open for {{ s.application_days }} days. You can change your application at any time until the deadline.
  3. When the time is up, the round is evaluated and distributed exactly once, automatically.
  4. Everyone who applied gets an Auth notification with the result. The full ranking is visible on the round page.
3. Who can apply?

The CEO{% if s.allow_directors %} or a director{% endif %} of an alliance corp – one application per corp. {% if s.allow_directors %}Directors are recognised via Corptools, so the character needs an active Corptools audit.{% endif %}

4. How to apply
  1. Open Moon Distribution → Rounds and click Apply next to your corp.
  2. Tick every moon your corp wants – up to {{ s.max_wishes }}. The moons are grouped in tabs per region; your selection counts across all tabs.
  3. Click a column header to sort (e.g. by rarity or by the share of the valuable ore), Shift+click adds a second sort level.
  4. Save. All ticked moons count the same – there is no ranking.

Colours: R32 R16 R8 R4. The chips next to a moon show its ores and their share, Best % is the share of the most valuable ore of the moon's class.

5. How corps are rated

Activity is measured for the period shown on the round (usually the last ~6 months). Each criterion is scaled from 0 to 100 against the best applying corp, then the weighted average is your score.

7. How the moons are assigned
  1. All applying corps are sorted by their total score (on a tie, the earlier application wins).
  2. In order, every corp gets one moon: the best of its ticked moons that is still free (rarity first, then share of the valuable ore).
  3. Then the next pass starts with the best corp again – until every corp has {{ s.max_moons_per_corp }} moons or none of its moons is free any more.

Limits per corp and round: {% if s.max_r32_per_corp is not None %}max. {{ s.max_r32_per_corp }} × R32{% endif %} {% if s.max_r16_per_corp is not None %}, max. {{ s.max_r16_per_corp }} × R16{% endif %} – if your corp reached a limit, the next free moon of a different class is taken instead.

8. Tips
  • Tick enough moons – if all of your moons are taken before it is your turn, your corp gets nothing.
  • Make sure all your members have registered their characters in the Auth – only registered players count as active members.
  • Fly fleets with FAT links and fight – that is what counts most.
